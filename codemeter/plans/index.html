FAQ

Questions.

Which plans does codeMeter work with?

Claude Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise, and ChatGPT plans that include Codex, such as Plus and Pro. ChatGPT Business and Enterprise workspaces have not been verified yet.

Does it work with the free Claude or ChatGPT plan?

No. codeMeter shows your plan's usage limits, so you need a paid plan for each account you show.

What happens if my plan's limits change?

The screen shows whatever limits your plan reports. When Claude Pro and Max moved to a weekly limit only on October 30, 2026, every codeMeter updated itself the next day.

What do I need?

A paid plan for each account you show (Claude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise, or a ChatGPT plan with Codex), 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with a WPA2 password, and, for Claude, a computer once per account to run claude setup-token.
