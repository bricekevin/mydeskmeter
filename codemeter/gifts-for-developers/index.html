Before you buy

Three things to check.

  • A paid planClaude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise, or a ChatGPT plan that includes Codex. It does not include any AI service.
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-FiStandard home Wi-Fi with a WPA2 password. Not 5 GHz-only or WPA3-only.
  • Claude or Codex?You do not need to know. They pick at setup, and can change it later with a reset.

Make it theirs

Colour and power.

Stands come in Black, Orange, Blue, Red, Wood, Gold, Rainbow and Glow in the dark, 3D-printed to order in Encinitas, California. Choose USB-C, a standard battery, or an All Day battery. From $119.

In the box: the stand, a USB-C cable and a setup card. Setup takes about five minutes.

FAQ

Questions.

Do they need anything else?

A paid plan, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, their phone, and for Claude a computer once to run claude setup-token.

Is there a subscription to keep paying?

No. Updates are free and install themselves over Wi-Fi.

What if they use both Claude and Codex?

They can pick Claude + Codex at setup and see both on one screen.

Can it be personalised?

The stand colour, and up to two account names of 10 characters each. For a company logo on the start-up screen, see team rollouts.
