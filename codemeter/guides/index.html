FAQ

Questions.

Where is the step-by-step setup guide?

There is a printed card in the box and a guided page at encinitas3d.com/codemeter. The Claude and Codex setup guides here cover the same steps.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes, from plugging it in to live numbers.

Does my computer need to stay on?

No. After setup codeMeter runs on its own over Wi-Fi and checks every 60 seconds. Your computer can be off or closed.
