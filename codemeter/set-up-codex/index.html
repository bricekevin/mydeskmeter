Steps

Three steps.

  1. 1Plug it in

    Join the codeMeter-XXXXXX Wi-Fi from your phone and tap Setup Device.

  2. 2Pick OpenAI Codex

    Choose your home Wi-Fi, enter its password and Save. No token, no computer.

  3. 3Type the code

    The device restarts and shows a code and a QR. Open auth.openai.com/codex/device, sign in to ChatGPT and type the code.

FAQ

Questions.

Do I need a computer or the Codex CLI?

No. Codex setup is done entirely on your phone.

The code expired. Now what?

Codes are valid for 15 minutes. When one expires, press B for a new code.

How long does the sign-in last?

The device renews it automatically, about every 10 days. If it lapses the screen says Codex login expired: hold B about 7 seconds and sign in again. Your Wi-Fi is kept.

Does it see my ChatGPT password?

No. You sign in on OpenAI's own page on your phone. The device stores only its own sign-in, on the device.
