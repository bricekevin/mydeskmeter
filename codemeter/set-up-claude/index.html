Setup guide

Set up with Claude.

About five minutes. You need your phone, and a computer once to run claude setup-token.

Build yours From $119

Steps

Three steps.

  1. 1Plug it in

    Join the codeMeter-XXXXXX Wi-Fi from your phone. The setup page opens (or visit http://192.168.4.1).

  2. 2Get your token

    On a computer with the Claude CLI, run claude setup-token. It opens a browser to sign in, then prints a long token.

  3. 3Paste and save

    Tap Setup Device, pick Claude Code, paste the token (or scan the QR from the pairing page), choose your home Wi-Fi and Save.

The token

What claude setup-token does.

It is one command in Anthropic's Claude CLI (a free download). It signs you in through your browser and prints a long-lived token. codeMeter uses that token to read your usage. You do it once per Claude account.

The pairing page at encinitas3d.com/codemeter turns the token into a QR code in your browser, so you can scan it from the device's setup page instead of pasting. Nothing is uploaded.

FAQ

Questions.

Do I need the Claude CLI?

Yes, once per Claude account, to run claude setup-token. It is a free download from Anthropic. After that the device runs on its own.

Is the token sent to you?

No. You paste it over the device's own local Wi-Fi, and the pairing page builds its QR in your browser. The token is stored on the device only.

The screen says Claude login expired. What now?

Hold B about 7 seconds (an orange screen appears at 5 seconds, keep holding) and paste a fresh token from claude setup-token. Your Wi-Fi and names are kept.

Which Wi-Fi does it need?

2.4 GHz with a WPA2 password. It does not join 5 GHz-only or WPA3-only networks.
