FAQ

Questions.

Does codeMeter need software on my computer?

No. You need a computer once per Claude account to run claude setup-token; Codex needs no computer at all. After that nothing runs on your computer.

Where is my sign-in stored?

On the device only. There is no account with us and no server of ours in between.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy the device once. Firmware updates are free and install themselves over Wi-Fi; if an update ever fails to start, it rolls back to the previous version.
