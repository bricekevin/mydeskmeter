How updates work

Nothing to do.

  • AutomaticWhile it is plugged in and on Wi-Fi, it checks for new firmware every 6 hours and installs it by itself.
  • SafeIf an update ever fails to start, it rolls back to the previous version.
  • FreeNo subscription. New features, like two accounts, simply appear.
  • Your versionHold A for the Status screen to see which version you have.

FAQ

Questions.

Does my codeMeter update itself?

Yes. It checks every 6 hours while plugged in and on Wi-Fi, and installs new firmware by itself, free.

Do I need to turn on new features?

Most appear by themselves. Two Claude accounts is a setup choice: hold B about 7 seconds and pick 2 Claude accounts.

What if an update goes wrong?

If it fails to start, the device rolls back to the previous version. A USB recovery page is at bricekevin.github.io/fw.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy the device once. Firmware updates are free and install themselves over Wi-Fi; if an update ever fails to start, it rolls back to the previous version.
