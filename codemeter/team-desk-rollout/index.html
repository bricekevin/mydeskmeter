FAQ

Questions.

Can you put our logo on it?

Yes. We make a custom start-up screen with your logo and colours, and stands to match.

Does each person need their own plan?

Yes. Each codeMeter shows the usage of the accounts signed in to it, so each person needs their own Claude or ChatGPT plan.

Does it need IT to install anything?

No. It joins 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with a WPA2 password and connects out to Anthropic or OpenAI, GitHub for updates, time servers and ip-api.com for the time zone. Nothing runs on anyone's computer.

How do we get a quote?

Use the quote form on the codeMeter page, or email info@encinitas3d.com with the company, quantity and colour.
