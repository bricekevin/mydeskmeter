FAQ

Questions.

Which crypto can priceMeter show?

Any pair Binance.US lists, typed as one word: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT and hundreds more.

Does it work outside the US?

Often, but Binance.US is blocked in some countries and on some networks. If this page shows the chart live, your network can reach it.

How fast does crypto update?

Live: the device keeps a stream open to Binance.US, so the last candle moves within seconds of a trade.

What if Binance.US delists my coin?

The screen stops updating for that pair. Hold the middle button at start-up to reopen setup and pick another.
