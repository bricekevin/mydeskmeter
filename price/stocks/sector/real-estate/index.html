FAQ

Questions.

Can I see Real Estate stock prices on a desk display?

Yes. Add up to 25 tickers to priceMeter, like Stock:WELL, and it shows each live price with a candlestick chart.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. Stocks keep their last price and chart until trading opens again.

How fast do Real Estate stocks update?

Every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open; the chart reloads every 30 seconds.
