FAQ

Questions.

Can I see Northern Trust price on a desk display?

Yes. priceMeter shows NTRS live on a small Wi-Fi screen: the price, a 24-candle chart and the move across it. Type Stock:NTRS during setup.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. NTRS trades on Nasdaq during US market hours. Outside them the screen keeps the last price and chart until trading opens again. Crypto on the same device keeps moving around the clock.

How fast does NTRS update?

The price refreshes every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open, and the whole chart every 30 seconds. No API key or brokerage account is needed.

Can I show NTRS with other tickers?

Yes, up to 25 in any mix of stocks, ETFs and crypto. Press the left button for the next one, or left and right together to cycle every 15 seconds.
