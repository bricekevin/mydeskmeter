Stock:FDS

Give it a short name, up to 8 letters, like FactSet. Upper or lower case both work. The device sends FDS to Yahoo Finance as typed.

    Join the SETUP PRICE TICKER Wi-Fi from your phone. The setup page opens.

    Pick your home Wi-Fi, then type Stock:FDS in a ticker field and FactSet as its name.

  3. 3Watch it go

FAQ

Questions.

Can I see FactSet price on a desk display?

Yes. priceMeter shows FDS live on a small Wi-Fi screen: the price, a 24-candle chart and the move across it. Type Stock:FDS during setup.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. FDS trades on the NYSE during US market hours. Outside them the screen keeps the last price and chart until trading opens again. Crypto on the same device keeps moving around the clock.

How fast does FDS update?

The price refreshes every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open, and the whole chart every 30 seconds. No API key or brokerage account is needed.

Can I show FDS with other tickers?

Yes, up to 25 in any mix of stocks, ETFs and crypto. Press the left button for the next one, or left and right together to cycle every 15 seconds.
