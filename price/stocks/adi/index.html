FAQ

Questions.

Can I see Analog Devices price on a desk display?

Yes. priceMeter shows ADI live on a small Wi-Fi screen: the price, a 24-candle chart and the move across it. Type Stock:ADI during setup.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. ADI trades on Nasdaq during US market hours. Outside them the screen keeps the last price and chart until trading opens again. Crypto on the same device keeps moving around the clock.

How fast does ADI update?

The price refreshes every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open, and the whole chart every 30 seconds. No API key or brokerage account is needed.

Can I show ADI with other tickers?

Yes, up to 25 in any mix of stocks, ETFs and crypto. Press the left button for the next one, or left and right together to cycle every 15 seconds.
