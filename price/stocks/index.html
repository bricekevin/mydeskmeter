FAQ

Questions.

Which stocks can priceMeter show?

Any US-listed stock or ETF that Yahoo Finance charts, typed as Stock: and the ticker, like Stock:AAPL. That covers every S&P 500 company and thousands more.

How do I type class shares like Berkshire B?

Use a dash, like Stock:BRK-B. Yahoo Finance answers BRK-B, not BRK.B or BRK/B.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. Stocks keep their last price and chart until trading opens again.

How fast do stock prices update?

Every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open; the chart reloads every 30 seconds.
