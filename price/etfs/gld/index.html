About

SPDR Gold Shares, on your desk.

SPDR Gold Shares holds physical gold, so it follows the gold price. priceMeter shows it next to up to 24 other stocks, ETFs or crypto pairs, in any mix.

FAQ

Questions.

Can I see SPDR Gold Shares price on a desk display?

Yes. priceMeter shows GLD live on a small Wi-Fi screen: the price, a 24-candle chart and the move across it. Type Stock:GLD during setup.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. GLD trades on NYSE Arca during US market hours. Outside them the screen keeps the last price and chart until trading opens again. Crypto on the same device keeps moving around the clock.

How fast does GLD update?

The price refreshes every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open, and the whole chart every 30 seconds. No API key or brokerage account is needed.

Can I show GLD with other tickers?

Yes, up to 25 in any mix of stocks, ETFs and crypto. Press the left button for the next one, or left and right together to cycle every 15 seconds.
