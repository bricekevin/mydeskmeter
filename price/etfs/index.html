FAQ

Questions.

Can priceMeter show ETFs?

Yes. Type the ETF like a stock, Stock:SPY or Stock:QQQ. Yahoo Finance charts ETFs the same way.

Can I see a bitcoin ETF and bitcoin itself?

Yes. Add Stock:IBIT for the ETF in market hours and BTCUSDT for bitcoin around the clock.

How fast do ETF prices update?

Every 15 seconds while the market is open; outside market hours they keep the last price and chart.
