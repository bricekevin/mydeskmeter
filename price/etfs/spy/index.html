Stock:SPY

Give it a short name, up to 8 letters, like SPY. Upper or lower case both work. The device sends SPY to Yahoo Finance as typed.

  1. 1Plug it in

    Join the SETUP PRICE TICKER Wi-Fi from your phone. The setup page opens.

  2. 2Type the ticker

    Pick your home Wi-Fi, then type Stock:SPY in a ticker field and SPY as its name.

  3. 3Watch it go

    It joins your Wi-Fi and draws the chart. Press the left button for your next ticker.

FAQ

Questions.

Can I see SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust price on a desk display?

Yes. priceMeter shows SPY live on a small Wi-Fi screen: the price, a 24-candle chart and the move across it. Type Stock:SPY during setup.

Does it work when the market is closed?

Yes. SPY trades on NYSE Arca during US market hours. Outside them the screen keeps the last price and chart until trading opens again. Crypto on the same device keeps moving around the clock.

How fast does SPY update?

The price refreshes every 15 seconds from Yahoo Finance while the market is open, and the whole chart every 30 seconds. No API key or brokerage account is needed.

Can I show SPY with other tickers?

Yes, up to 25 in any mix of stocks, ETFs and crypto. Press the left button for the next one, or left and right together to cycle every 15 seconds.
