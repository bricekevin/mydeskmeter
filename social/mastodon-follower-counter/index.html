What it shows for Mastodon.

The labels below are the ones printed on the device.

FOLLOWERS
Your follower count, as your own Mastodon server reports it.
POSTS
Posts (toots) on your account.
FOLLOWING
Accounts you follow.
N TODAY
The green pill: followers gained since the day's first reading, in your time zone.
NEXT MILESTONE
A bar to the next round number (1, 2 or 5 followed by zeros), with how far along you are.
Circle and dot
The circle empties until the next check. The dot is green while the number is live, amber while it shows the last reading, red when something needs a look.

Checks: Every 60 seconds.

What you need

Set up in a few minutes.

Name only

It needs only your full handle: no key, no login. Your full handle stays on the device. No account with us, no app.

  1. Find your full handle
    Your username plus your server, joined by @: user@instance.exampleyou@mastodon.social
  2. Plug it in and join its Wi-Fi
    Power it with any USB-C cable. Scan the code on its screen (or join the Social Meter-XXXXXX network) and the setup page opens. If it does not, visit 192.168.4.1.
  3. Choose Mastodon and fill in one page
    Pick Mastodon from the list and type your full handle. Leave the API-key box blank.
  4. Pick your home Wi-Fi and save
    Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, type its password and tap Save. It restarts and shows your number within about 30 seconds.

FAQ

Questions.

Does it work with any Mastodon server?

Yes. It asks your own server, the part after the @, so any public instance works. Type the full handle, like you@mastodon.social.

Why does my count differ from what someone sees on another server?

Other servers only know the followers they have seen. The device asks your home server, which has the full number.

Do I need a key?

No. Mastodon profiles are public. It needs only your handle.

Does it need my Mastodon password?

No. Mastodon followers are public, so it needs only the full handle.
