On the screen

What it shows for Etsy.

The labels below are the ones printed on the device.

SALES
Your shop's total sales, the number Etsy shows on your shop page.
ADMIRERS
Admirers: people who have favourited your shop.
REVIEWS
Reviews on your shop.
N TODAY
The green pill: shop sales gained since the day's first reading, in your time zone.
NEXT MILESTONE
A bar to the next round number (1, 2 or 5 followed by zeros), with how far along you are.
Circle and dot
The circle empties until the next check. The dot is green while the number is live, amber while it shows the last reading, red when something needs a look.

Checks: Every 60 seconds.

What you need

Set up in a few minutes.

Needs a free key

It needs your Etsy shop name and a free Etsy keystring and shared secret you make once. Your Etsy shop name and key stay on the device. No account with us, no app.

  1. Find your Etsy shop name
    Your public Etsy shop name, as it appears in your shop link.exampleYourShop
  2. Get your Etsy keystring and shared secret
    1. Go to etsy.com/developers and create a Seller App. Approval is instant for an active shop.
    2. From the app, copy both the Keystring and the Shared secret.
    3. Paste them into the key box joined by a colon: keystring:shared_secret.
  3. Plug it in and join its Wi-Fi
    Power it with any USB-C cable. Scan the code on its screen (or join the Social Meter-XXXXXX network) and the setup page opens. If it does not, visit 192.168.4.1.
  4. Choose Etsy and fill in one page
    Pick Etsy from the list and type your Etsy shop name, then paste your key into the API-key box.
  5. Pick your home Wi-Fi and save
    Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, type its password and tap Save. It restarts and shows your number within about 30 seconds.

FAQ

Questions.

Does it show revenue?

No. It shows the sales count, admirers and reviews: the public numbers on your shop. No money figures.

Is my Etsy key safe?

It stays on the device, in its own storage, and goes only to Etsy. There is no account with us and no server in between.

Does it ring or flash on a new sale?

No sound. The number goes up and the green today pill grows within a minute of the sale.

Does it need my Etsy password?

No. It reads the count with the free key you make, never your password. The key stays on the device.
