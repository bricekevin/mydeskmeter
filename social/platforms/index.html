FAQ

Questions.

Which platforms need an API key?

YouTube, YouTube Live, Twitch, Kick, Spotify, Telegram, Last.fm, Etsy and Shopify take a free key you make once. Bluesky, Mastodon, GitHub, npm, Docker Hub, Discord, Discourse, Steam, Roblox, Minecraft and Wikipedia need only a name.

Can it show more than one platform?

One at a time. Hold the middle button for about seven seconds and pick again on your phone. Your Wi-Fi is kept.

Will more platforms be added?

Platforms arrive in free updates that install themselves over Wi-Fi. Eight came in one update in September 2026.

Why not Instagram, TikTok or X?

Instagram and TikTok only share follower counts with reviewed apps through each user's sign-in, and X has no free API. A device that asks the platform directly cannot do that, so we leave them out.
