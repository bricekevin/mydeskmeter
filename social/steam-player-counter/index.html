On the screen

What it shows for Steam.

The labels below are the ones printed on the device.

PLAYERS NOW
Concurrent players in the game right now, from Steam's public player-count API.
REVIEWS
Total user reviews.
POSITIVE
Positive reviews.
No today pill
Players right now rise and fall, so the device leaves out a daily gain.
NEXT MILESTONE
A bar to the next round number (1, 2 or 5 followed by zeros), with how far along you are.
Circle and dot
The circle empties until the next check. The dot is green while the number is live, amber while it shows the last reading, red when something needs a look.

Checks: Every 60 seconds.

What you need

Set up in a few minutes.

Name only

It needs only your Steam app ID: no key, no login. Your Steam app ID stays on the device. No account with us, no app.

  1. Find your Steam app ID
    The number in the game's store link, like store.steampowered.com/app/570.example570
  2. Plug it in and join its Wi-Fi
    Power it with any USB-C cable. Scan the code on its screen (or join the Social Meter-XXXXXX network) and the setup page opens. If it does not, visit 192.168.4.1.
  3. Choose Steam and fill in one page
    Pick Steam from the list and type your Steam app ID. Leave the API-key box blank.
  4. Pick your home Wi-Fi and save
    Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, type its password and tap Save. It restarts and shows your number within about 30 seconds.

FAQ

Questions.

Do I need a Steam Web API key?

No. Current player counts and review totals are public, so the app ID is enough.

Can I watch any game, not just mine?

Yes. Any game with a Steam store page works. Type its app ID.

Why no today number?

Players rise and fall all day, so the device shows the count right now and leaves out a daily gain.

Does it need my Steam password?

No. Steam players right now are public, so it needs only the Steam app ID.
