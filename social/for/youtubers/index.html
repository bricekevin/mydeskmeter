FAQ

Questions.

Can it show subscribers and live viewers together?

One at a time. Hold the middle button for about seven seconds to pick again; your Wi-Fi is kept. Many streamers keep a second one for the other count.

Why does my subscriber count move in steps?

YouTube rounds subscriber counts to three significant figures for every channel. The device shows exactly what YouTube reports.

Is the API key hard to get?

It takes a few minutes in Google Cloud, with no billing card. The YouTube page below walks through it step by step.
