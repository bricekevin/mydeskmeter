FAQ

Questions.

Does it show revenue?

No. It shows counts: sales or orders, plus admirers, reviews, products or customers. No money figures on the screen.

Is it safe to give it my shop key?

The key stays in the device's own storage and goes only to Etsy or your Shopify store. There is no account with us and no server in between. Shopify's token needs read access only.

Can it show two shops?

One at a time. To switch, hold the middle button for about seven seconds and pick again on your phone.
