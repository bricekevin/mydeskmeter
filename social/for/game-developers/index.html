FAQ

Questions.

Do any of these need a key?

No. Steam, Roblox, Minecraft and Discord all give player or online counts publicly.

Why is there no today number?

Players come and go all day, so the device shows the count right now and leaves out a daily gain.

Which Roblox number do I need?

The universe ID, from Creator Dashboard. The number in the game's link is a place ID and will not work.
