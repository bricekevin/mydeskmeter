FAQ

Questions.

Does it show Spotify monthly listeners?

No. Spotify's API offers followers and a popularity score, not monthly listeners.

Do I need a Spotify for Artists login?

No. You create a free developer app and paste its client ID and secret. Any artist's public follower count works.

Does it play music?

No. It is a display: one number, refreshed every minute.
