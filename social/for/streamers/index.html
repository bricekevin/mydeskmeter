FAQ

Questions.

Does it slow down my stream or PC?

No. It is its own little computer on your Wi-Fi and asks the platform directly. Nothing to install on your PC.

How quickly does it update?

Every 60 seconds, the same pace on every platform.

Can it show chat or follows?

No. It shows one number, viewers right now, with Kick's subscribers or YouTube's views underneath.
