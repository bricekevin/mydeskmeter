On the screen

What it shows for Last.fm.

The labels below are the ones printed on the device.

SCROBBLES
Your total scrobbles: every track Last.fm has recorded you playing.
ARTISTS
Different artists you have scrobbled.
TRACKS
Different tracks you have scrobbled.
N TODAY
The green pill: scrobbles gained since the day's first reading, in your time zone.
NEXT MILESTONE
A bar to the next round number (1, 2 or 5 followed by zeros), with how far along you are.
Circle and dot
The circle empties until the next check. The dot is green while the number is live, amber while it shows the last reading, red when something needs a look.

Checks: Every 60 seconds.

What you need

Set up in a few minutes.

Needs a free key

It needs your Last.fm username and a free Last.fm API key you make once. Your Last.fm username and key stay on the device. No account with us, no app.

  1. Find your Last.fm username
    Your Last.fm username, exactly as it appears in your profile link.exampleyourname
  2. Get your Last.fm API key
    1. Go to last.fm/api/account/create.
    2. Fill in any application name. The other fields can stay blank.
    3. Copy the API key (not the shared secret) into the key box.
  3. Plug it in and join its Wi-Fi
    Power it with any USB-C cable. Scan the code on its screen (or join the Social Meter-XXXXXX network) and the setup page opens. If it does not, visit 192.168.4.1.
  4. Choose Last.fm and fill in one page
    Pick Last.fm from the list and type your Last.fm username, then paste your key into the API-key box.
  5. Pick your home Wi-Fi and save
    Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, type its password and tap Save. It restarts and shows your number within about 30 seconds.

FAQ

Questions.

How fast does it count a new scrobble?

On the next check, within a minute of Last.fm recording it. The today pill shows how many you have scrobbled since the day began.

Is the Last.fm API key free?

Yes. Creating an API account takes a minute and needs only an application name.

Can it show someone else's scrobbles?

Yes, any public profile. Type their username instead.

Does it need my Last.fm password?

No. It reads the count with the free key you make, never your password. The key stays on the device.
