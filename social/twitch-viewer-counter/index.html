On the screen

What it shows for Twitch.

The labels below are the ones printed on the device.

VIEWERS NOW
Concurrent viewers on the channel's live stream, as Twitch reports it. It reads 0 while the channel is offline.
No today pill
Viewers right now rise and fall, so the device leaves out a daily gain.
NEXT MILESTONE
A bar to the next round number (1, 2 or 5 followed by zeros), with how far along you are.
Circle and dot
The circle empties until the next check. The dot is green while the number is live, amber while it shows the last reading, red when something needs a look.

Checks: Every 60 seconds.

What you need

Set up in a few minutes.

Needs a free key

It needs your channel name and a free Twitch client ID and client secret you make once. Your channel name and key stay on the device. No account with us, no app.

  1. Find your channel name
    The channel's login name, the part after twitch.tv/. Shows 0 when the channel is offline.exampleyourstream
  2. Get your Twitch client ID and client secret
    1. Go to dev.twitch.tv/console and choose Register Your Application.
    2. Set the OAuth Redirect URL to http://localhost and the category to Other.
    3. Copy the Client ID, then press New Secret to get a Client Secret.
    4. Paste them into the key box joined by a colon: client_id:client_secret.
  3. Plug it in and join its Wi-Fi
    Power it with any USB-C cable. Scan the code on its screen (or join the Social Meter-XXXXXX network) and the setup page opens. If it does not, visit 192.168.4.1.
  4. Choose Twitch and fill in one page
    Pick Twitch from the list and type your channel name, then paste your key into the API-key box.
  5. Pick your home Wi-Fi and save
    Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, type its password and tap Save. It restarts and shows your number within about 30 seconds.

FAQ

Questions.

Does it show followers or subscribers?

Live viewers right now. That is the number it reads from Twitch's streams API every minute, and the one streamers glance at during a stream.

Do I have to log in with my Twitch account?

No. You register a free app on Twitch's developer console and paste its client ID and secret. It never asks for your Twitch password, and it can show any public channel.

Can it count someone else's channel?

Yes. Viewer counts are public, so any channel name works, as long as you type it as it appears in the twitch.tv link.

Does it need my Twitch password?

No. It reads the count with the free key you make, never your password. The key stays on the device.
