What it shows for Roblox.

The labels below are the ones printed on the device.

PLAYERS NOW
Players in the experience right now, across all its servers.
VISITS

FAVOURITES
Favourites.
No today pill
Players right now rise and fall, so the device leaves out a daily gain.
NEXT MILESTONE
A bar to the next round number (1, 2 or 5 followed by zeros), with how far along you are.
Circle and dot
The circle empties until the next check. The dot is green while the number is live, amber while it shows the last reading, red when something needs a look.

Checks: Every 60 seconds.

What you need

Set up in a few minutes.

Name only

It needs only your universe ID: no key, no login. Your universe ID stays on the device. No account with us, no app.

  1. Find your universe ID
    Your experience's universe ID. This is not the number in the game's link: that one is a place ID and will not work.example1234567890Find the universe ID in Creator Dashboard: open your experience, and it is in the address of its Configure page.
  2. Place ID or universe ID
    1. A link like roblox.com/games/4657294156/... holds a place ID.
    2. The universe (experience) ID is a different number, and it is the one to type.
  3. Plug it in and join its Wi-Fi
    Power it with any USB-C cable. Scan the code on its screen (or join the Social Meter-XXXXXX network) and the setup page opens. If it does not, visit 192.168.4.1.
  4. Choose Roblox and fill in one page
    Pick Roblox from the list and type your universe ID. Leave the API-key box blank.
  5. Pick your home Wi-Fi and save
    Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, type its password and tap Save. It restarts and shows your number within about 30 seconds.

FAQ

Questions.

It says not found. Why?

Almost always because the number is a place ID, from the game's link. The device needs the universe ID, shown in Creator Dashboard on the experience's Configure page.

Do I need a Roblox key?

No. Player counts, visits and favourites are public.

Can I watch someone else's experience?

Yes, any public experience, once you know its universe ID.

Does it need my Roblox password?

No. Roblox players right now are public, so it needs only the universe ID.
