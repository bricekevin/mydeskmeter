FAQ

Questions

Which one should I get?

Pick the number you check most: AI coding limits (codeMeter), stock and crypto prices (priceMeter), live scores (sportsMeter) or follower counts (socialMeter).

Is it the same hardware?

Yes. Every meter is the same small Wi-Fi display on a 3D-printed stand. The software is what makes each one a meter.

Is there a subscription?

No. Each meter updates itself over Wi-Fi for free.

What Wi-Fi do I need?

2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with a password (WPA2). Most home and office networks have it.

Battery or plug-in?

Every meter comes as USB-C plug-in, and most colours also come with a built-in battery.
