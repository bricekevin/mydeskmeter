FAQ

Questions.

Can sportsMeter follow every Kansas City team at once?

Yes. Kansas City has 4 teams here and sportsMeter follows up to six, so all of them fit on one screen.

What happens when two Kansas City teams play at the same time?

Live games take the screen and take turns with each other every 12 seconds until they end. Double tap the left button to stay on one.

How do I set it up for Kansas City teams?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then add each team: choose its league, then the team, and Save.
