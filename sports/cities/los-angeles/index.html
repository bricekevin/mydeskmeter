FAQ

Questions.

Can sportsMeter follow every Los Angeles team at once?

It follows up to six teams. Los Angeles has 12 here, so pick your six; you can change them any time by holding the middle button for 2 seconds.

What happens when two Los Angeles teams play at the same time?

Live games take the screen and take turns with each other every 12 seconds until they end. Double tap the left button to stay on one.

How do I set it up for Los Angeles teams?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then add each team: choose its league, then the team, and Save.
