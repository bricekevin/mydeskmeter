On the screen

What a MLS team looks like.

  • The season so far

    Record and place in the standings, under the team's logo and in its colours.

  • The next game

    Opponent, kick-off time in your time zone and TV. On game day the card lights up.

  • Live games

    The score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds.

  • The numbers

    Points, goals for and against, home and away records.

FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every MLS team?

Yes, all 30 teams listed here. At setup choose MLS as the league, then your team.

Does it show MLS games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow MLS teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
