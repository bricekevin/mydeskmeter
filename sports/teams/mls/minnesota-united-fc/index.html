FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Minnesota United FC scores live?

Yes. During a Minnesota United FC game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Minnesota United FC?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose MLS as the league and Minnesota United FC as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Minnesota United FC and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Minnesota Vikings and Minnesota Twins. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Minnesota United FC logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#000000 and #9bcde4). Until the logo arrives it shows MIN on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Minnesota United FC matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
