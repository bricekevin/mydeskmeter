FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Philadelphia Union scores live?

Yes. During a Philadelphia Union game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Philadelphia Union?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose MLS as the league and Philadelphia Union as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Philadelphia Union and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Philadelphia Eagles and Philadelphia Phillies. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Philadelphia Union logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#051f31 and #e0d0a6). Until the logo arrives it shows PHI on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Philadelphia Union matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
