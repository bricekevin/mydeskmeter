FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Red Bull New York scores live?

Yes. During a Red Bull New York game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Red Bull New York?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose MLS as the league and Red Bull New York as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Red Bull New York and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example New York Giants and New York Jets. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Red Bull New York logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#ba0c2f and #ffc72c). Until the logo arrives it shows RBNY on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Red Bull New York matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
