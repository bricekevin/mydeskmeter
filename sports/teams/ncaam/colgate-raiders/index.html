FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Colgate Raiders basketball scores live?

Yes. During a Colgate Raiders game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Colgate Raiders?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College BB as the league and Colgate Raiders as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Colgate Raiders and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Chattanooga Mocs and Chicago State Cougars. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Colgate Raiders logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#821019 and #ffffff). Until the logo arrives it shows COLG on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Colgate Raiders are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its tip-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
