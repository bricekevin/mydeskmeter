FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Marist Red Foxes basketball scores live?

Yes. During a Marist Red Foxes game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Marist Red Foxes?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College BB as the league and Marist Red Foxes as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Marist Red Foxes and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Loyola Maryland Greyhounds and Loyola Marymount Lions. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Marist Red Foxes logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#e53730 and #f0f0f0). Until the logo arrives it shows MRST on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Marist Red Foxes are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its tip-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
