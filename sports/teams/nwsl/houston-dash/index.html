FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Houston Dash scores live?

Yes. During a Houston Dash game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Houston Dash?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose NWSL as the league and Houston Dash as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Houston Dash and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Houston Texans and Houston Astros. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Houston Dash logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#ff6900 and #8ab7e9). Until the logo arrives it shows HOU on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Houston Dash matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
