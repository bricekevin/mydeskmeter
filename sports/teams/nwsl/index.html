FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every NWSL team?

Yes, all 16 teams listed here. At setup choose NWSL as the league, then your team.

Does it show NWSL games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow NWSL teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
