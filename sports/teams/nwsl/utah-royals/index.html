FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Utah Royals scores live?

Yes. During a Utah Royals game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Utah Royals?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose NWSL as the league and Utah Royals as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Utah Royals and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Utah Jazz and Utah Mammoth. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Utah Royals logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#ae122a and #fdb71a). Until the logo arrives it shows UTA on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Utah Royals matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
