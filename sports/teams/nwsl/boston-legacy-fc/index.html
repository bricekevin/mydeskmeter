FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Boston Legacy FC scores live?

Yes. During a Boston Legacy FC game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Boston Legacy FC?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose NWSL as the league and Boston Legacy FC as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Boston Legacy FC and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example New England Patriots and Boston Red Sox. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Boston Legacy FC logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#00ff00 and #000000). Until the logo arrives it shows BOS on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Boston Legacy FC matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
