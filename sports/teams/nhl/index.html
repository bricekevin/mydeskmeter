On the screen

What a NHL team looks like.

  • The season so far

    Record and place in the standings, under the team's logo and in its colours.

  • The next game

    Opponent, puck drop time in your time zone and TV. On game day the card lights up.

  • Live games

    The score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds.

  • The numbers

    Points, goals for and against.

FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every NHL team?

Yes, all 32 teams listed here. At setup choose NHL as the league, then your team.

Does it show NHL games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow NHL teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
