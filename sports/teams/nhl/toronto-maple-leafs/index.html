FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Toronto Maple Leafs scores live?

Yes. During a Toronto Maple Leafs game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Toronto Maple Leafs?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose NHL as the league and Toronto Maple Leafs as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Toronto Maple Leafs and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Toronto Blue Jays and Toronto Raptors. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Toronto Maple Leafs logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#003e7e and #ffffff). Until the logo arrives it shows TOR on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Toronto Maple Leafs are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its puck drop time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
