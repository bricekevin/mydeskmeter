FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Boston Celtics scores live?

Yes. During a Boston Celtics game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Boston Celtics?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose NBA as the league and Boston Celtics as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Boston Celtics and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example New England Patriots and Boston Red Sox. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Boston Celtics logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#008348 and #ffffff). Until the logo arrives it shows BOS on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Boston Celtics are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its tip-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
