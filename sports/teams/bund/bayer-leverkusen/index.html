FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Bayer Leverkusen scores live?

Yes. During a Bayer Leverkusen game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Bayer Leverkusen?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose Bundesliga as the league and Bayer Leverkusen as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Bayer Leverkusen and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example 1. FC Union Berlin and Bayern Munich. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Bayer Leverkusen logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#da0308 and #f9fbfc). Until the logo arrives it shows B04 on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Bayer Leverkusen matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
