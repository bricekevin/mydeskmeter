FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show New England Patriots scores live?

Yes. During a New England Patriots game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the New England Patriots?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose NFL as the league and New England Patriots as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the New England Patriots and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Boston Red Sox and Boston Celtics. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the New England Patriots logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#002a5c and #c60c30). Until the logo arrives it shows NE on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the New England Patriots are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kickoff time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
