    Opponent, kickoff time in your time zone and TV. On game day the card lights up.

    Win or loss streak, points for and against, home and away records.

FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every NFL team?

Yes, all 32 teams listed here. At setup choose NFL as the league, then your team.

Does it show NFL games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow NFL teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
