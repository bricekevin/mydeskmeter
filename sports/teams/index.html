FAQ

Questions.

Which leagues can sportsMeter follow?

NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, WNBA, MLS, NWSL, college football, men's and women's college basketball, the Premier League, Liga MX, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1: 1,785 teams in all.

Can I mix teams from different sports?

Yes. Pick up to six from any of the leagues. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over until it ends.

My team is not in the list. Can it be added?

The device follows every team the scores feed carries for these 16 leagues. If yours is missing, email info@encinitas3d.com.
