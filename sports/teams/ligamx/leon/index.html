FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show León scores live?

Yes. During a León game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for León?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose Liga MX as the league and León as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow León and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Atlante and Atlas. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the León logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#008000 and #fff61b). Until the logo arrives it shows LEO on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between León matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
