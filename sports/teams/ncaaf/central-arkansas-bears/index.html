FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Central Arkansas Bears football scores live?

Yes. During a Central Arkansas Bears game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Central Arkansas Bears?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College FB as the league and Central Arkansas Bears as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Central Arkansas Bears and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Carson Newman Eagles and Carthage Firebirds. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Central Arkansas Bears logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#a7a9ac and #8e959a). Until the logo arrives it shows CARK on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Central Arkansas Bears are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kickoff time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
