On the screen

What a College Football team looks like.

  • The season so far

    Record and place in the standings, under the team's logo and in its colours.

  • The next game

    Opponent, kickoff time in your time zone and TV. On game day the card lights up.

  • Live games

    The score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds.

  • The numbers

    Win or loss streak, points for and against, home and away records.

FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every College Football team?

Yes, all 761 teams listed here. At setup choose College FB as the league, then your team.

Does it show College Football games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow College Football teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
