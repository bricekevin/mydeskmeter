FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Idaho State Bengals football scores live?

Yes. During a Idaho State Bengals game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Idaho State Bengals?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College FB as the league and Idaho State Bengals as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Idaho State Bengals and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Houston Christian Huskies and Houston Cougars. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Idaho State Bengals logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#ef8c00 and #e9a126). Until the logo arrives it shows IDST on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Idaho State Bengals are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kickoff time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
