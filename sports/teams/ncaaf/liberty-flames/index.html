FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Liberty Flames football scores live?

Yes. During a Liberty Flames game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Liberty Flames?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College FB as the league and Liberty Flames as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Liberty Flames and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Langston Lions and Lawrence Vikings. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Liberty Flames logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#0a254e and #b72025). Until the logo arrives it shows LIB on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Liberty Flames are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kickoff time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
