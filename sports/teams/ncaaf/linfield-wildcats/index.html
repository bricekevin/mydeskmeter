FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Linfield Wildcats scores live?

Yes. During a Linfield Wildcats game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Linfield Wildcats?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College FB as the league and Linfield Wildcats as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Linfield Wildcats and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Lincoln (CA) Oaklanders and Lincoln (MO) Blue Tigers. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Linfield Wildcats logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#46256a and #d41e47). Until the logo arrives it shows LINF on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Linfield Wildcats are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kickoff time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
