FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show St. John Fisher Cardinals scores live?

Yes. During a St. John Fisher Cardinals game the screen shows the score, the clock and quarter, down and distance and who has the ball. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the St. John Fisher Cardinals?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College FB as the league and St. John Fisher Cardinals as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the St. John Fisher Cardinals and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Southwestern Oklahoma State Bulldogs and Southwestern U Pirates. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the St. John Fisher Cardinals logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#000000). Until the logo arrives it shows STJF on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the St. John Fisher Cardinals are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kickoff time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
