FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show William & Mary Tribe women's basketball scores live?

Yes. During a William & Mary Tribe game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the William & Mary Tribe?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College BB (W) as the league and William & Mary Tribe as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the William & Mary Tribe and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example West Virginia Mountaineers and Western Carolina Catamounts. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the William & Mary Tribe logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#115740 and #f0b323). Until the logo arrives it shows W&M on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the William & Mary Tribe are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its tip-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
