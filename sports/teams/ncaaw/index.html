On the screen

What a College Basketball (W) team looks like.

  • The season so far

    Record and place in the standings, under the team's logo and in its colours.

  • The next game

    Opponent, tip-off time in your time zone and TV. On game day the card lights up.

  • Live games

    The score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds.

  • The numbers

    Win or loss streak, points for and against, home and away records.

FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every College Basketball (W) team?

Yes, all 362 teams listed here. At setup choose College BB (W) as the league, then your team.

Does it show College Basketball (W) games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow College Basketball (W) teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
