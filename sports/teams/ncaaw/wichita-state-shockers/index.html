FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Wichita State Shockers women's basketball scores live?

Yes. During a Wichita State Shockers game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Wichita State Shockers?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College BB (W) as the league and Wichita State Shockers as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Wichita State Shockers and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example West Georgia Wolves and West Virginia Mountaineers. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Wichita State Shockers logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#ffcd00 and #27251f). Until the logo arrives it shows WICH on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Wichita State Shockers are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its tip-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
