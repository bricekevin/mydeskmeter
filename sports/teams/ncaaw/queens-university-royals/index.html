FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Queens University Royals scores live?

Yes. During a Queens University Royals game the screen shows the score with the clock and period. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the Queens University Royals?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose College BB (W) as the league and Queens University Royals as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the Queens University Royals and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example Prairie View A&M Lady Panthers and Presbyterian Blue Hose. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Queens University Royals logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#192c66 and #857040). Until the logo arrives it shows QUC on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the Queens University Royals are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its tip-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
