FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show San Diego Padres scores live?

Yes. During a San Diego Padres game the screen shows the score and inning, the count, outs and runners on base. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for the San Diego Padres?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose MLB as the league and San Diego Padres as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow the San Diego Padres and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example San Diego FC and San Diego Wave FC. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the San Diego Padres logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#2f241d and #ffc425). Until the logo arrives it shows SD on a block of the team colour.

What does it show when the San Diego Padres are not playing?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its first pitch time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
