On the screen

What a MLB team looks like.

  • The season so far

    Record and place in the standings, under the team's logo and in its colours.

  • The next game

    Opponent, first pitch time in your time zone and TV. On game day the card lights up.

  • Live games

    The score and inning, the count, outs and runners on base. It checks every 20 seconds.

  • The numbers

    Win or loss streak, runs scored and allowed, home and away records.

FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter cover every MLB team?

Yes, all 30 teams listed here. At setup choose MLB as the league, then your team.

Does it show MLB games live?

Yes. During a game the screen shows the score and inning, the count, outs and runners on base. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

Can I follow MLB teams with teams from other leagues?

Yes. Up to six teams from any mix of the 16 leagues take turns on the screen; a live game takes over until it ends.
