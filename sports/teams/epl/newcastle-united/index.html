FAQ

Questions.

Does sportsMeter show Newcastle United scores live?

Yes. During a Newcastle United game the screen shows the score and the match minute. It checks every 20 seconds, so it usually trails the action by under a minute.

How do I set it up for Newcastle United?

Plug it in, scan the code on the screen and pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Then choose Premier League as the league and Newcastle United as the team, and Save. No app or account.

Can I follow Newcastle United and other teams?

Yes, up to six teams from any of 16 leagues, for example AFC Bournemouth and Arsenal. They take turns every 12 seconds, and a live game takes over. Double tap the left button to stay on one team.

Does it show the Newcastle United logo and colours?

Yes. Once online it fetches the team logo and uses its colours (#000000 and #0b1b3d). Until the logo arrives it shows NEW on a block of the team colour.

What does it show between Newcastle United matches?

The record, place in the standings and the next game with its kick-off time. Between seasons the card reads "No game scheduled" until the next game is on the calendar.
